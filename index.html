Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about staying at RUMBA Hotel and Lodge in Gajuri, Dhading.

How do I reserve a room?

Choose a room, select your check-in and check-out dates, enter your name and phone number, and submit the request. The hotel will check availability and confirm it with you.

How is the room price calculated?

The estimate is the listed room price multiplied by the number of nights. Confirm the final price and any extra charges directly with the hotel.

Where is RUMBA Hotel and Lodge?

RUMBA Hotel and Lodge is in Gajuri, Dhading, Nepal. Use the Google Maps directions link on this website before travelling.

Is my booking confirmed when I submit the form?

No. A booking request remains pending until the hotel reviews it and marks it confirmed. Please contact the hotel if you need an immediate answer.
